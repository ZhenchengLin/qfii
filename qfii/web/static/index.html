A trading week, bottom up

Ten Nasdaq-100 stocks drawn at random are followed through one week of market time, every 10 minutes. Research rewrites a stock's report when something new happens (a filing, a price move, or a question). Assessment reads all ten reports, asks Research to dig deeper where it needs to, and takes the best to the committee, where it sits as the Neutral member with Aggressive and Conservative.
