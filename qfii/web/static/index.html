Watch three investors argue

Research gathers cited facts about a stock. Then the committee debates in rounds: Aggressive weighs upside, Conservative weighs downside, and Neutral weighs both against the S&P 500 and the whole portfolio. Each has one equal vote. They can invest, pass, ask a team for more information, or send the proposal back. A trade needs consensus on the action and the size; after three rounds without one, the stock is shelved.
